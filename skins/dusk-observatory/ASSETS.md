## 背景生成要求

Wide 16:9 fantasy collectible-card-game tabletop background for a desktop UI.
Dark desaturated navy slate and obsidian stone, subtle antique bronze inlays and
restrained cyan illumination; calm, low-contrast central play area. Fine arcane
astrolabe details around the outer corners, small warm candles near the edges,
and a faint distant gothic sanctuary toward the upper right. Premium, atmospheric,
physically textured, readable under cards. No cards, no characters, no text, no
logos, no UI, no buttons, no frames enclosing the hand or battlefield. Flat
top-down presentation, visual interest concentrated at the outer edges.
